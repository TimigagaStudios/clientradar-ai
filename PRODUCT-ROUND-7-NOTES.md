## What changed

- Assistant orb canvas stays within its stage and in document flow, rather than overlapping the briefing. The Lead Globe is a separate component and was not changed in this round.
- New demo generation optionally reads up to three additional prioritized same-origin HTML pages after a conservative robots.txt check. Successful extra reads contribute bounded, private page evidence; only allowlisted page types actually read become rough navigation cues to the AI. Extra page text is not used as business facts or as instructions, and is not used to populate lead contacts or images. The scan remains partial. A robots.txt result is not authorization or image reuse permission. Existing home, contact and about reads were not changed and are not robots-aware. New reads fail closed on unavailable or ambiguous policy, redirects and unsupported destinations; an explicit robots.txt 404 permits consideration of extra pages. Existing website safeguards and content limits still apply. Attempt timeouts are short and legitimate pages may be skipped. These additional requests may increase job time; serverless timing and live behavior must be monitored.
- New job paths default to Ultra Premium for missing or unrecognized tier; explicit Mid-Level and Hybrid remain supported. Existing stored jobs with missing tier retain their legacy Mid-Level interpretation. No automatic tier matching is added.
- Generated demos keep a labeled services placeholder when no services exist, restore subpage top anchors, and use cautious wording about unmarked image rights. Existing QA and approval gates are unchanged.
